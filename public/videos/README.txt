VIDEOS
======

intro.webm  - a 4 second PLACEHOLDER for the hero play button. Replace it, or point the admin
              (Hero -> Intro video) at your own link. Use a file here as /videos/intro.mp4 etc.
              For the best compatibility use MP4 (H.264 + AAC), 9:16 or 4:5, under 15 MB.
              A YouTube link or a Google Drive share link also works. Easiest: upload the video in the
              admin (Media tab, up to 20 MB), then use Hero -> Intro video -> Choose from Drive.

reel-*.mp4  - achievement reel videos (MP4, H.264, ideally under 15 MB each, 16:10 or 16:9).
              Names must match the "Achievements" reels in the admin or src/data/content.js, for example:

  reel-ncc-drill.mp4
  reel-iche.mp4
  reel-hackathon.mp4
  reel-marathon.mp4
  reel-internship.mp4

Reels play (muted, looping) when their slide is in the centre of the carousel.
